# Key Sources

The full source registry is available in [`data/source_registry.csv`](../data/source_registry.csv).

Primary source groups include:

- Apple country storefronts — iPhone 17 256GB listed prices
- European Central Bank — FX reference rates
- Apple India — GST-inclusive pricing policy
- World Bank WDI — household consumption expenditure per capita, PPP
- Government tax authorities — VAT/GST rates used in directional normalization
- Business Standard / MeitY reporting — India iPhone production, exports, value addition and component-import context
- Apple India Newsroom — historical India launch prices
- Samsung India Newsroom — Galaxy S26 256GB launch price

## Source discipline
This project does not treat modeled values as company facts. Tax normalization is directional and explicitly separated from Apple's own cost structure or margins.

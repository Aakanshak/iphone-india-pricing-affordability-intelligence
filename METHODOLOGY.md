# Methodology

## Research question
India has become a major iPhone manufacturing hub. Why can the same product still represent a much larger economic burden for an Indian consumer, and is sticker price the right way to evaluate that question?

## Analytical layers
1. Nominal cross-country pricing
2. Tax normalization
3. Consumer affordability
4. India manufacturing scale
5. Localization / value addition
6. Total cost of ownership

## Evidence labels
Every important figure is treated as one of:

- **REPORTED** — published by an official or cited public source
- **CALCULATED** — arithmetic based on reported data
- **ESTIMATED** — externally reported estimate or derived range
- **MODELED** — scenario assumption used for sensitivity analysis

## Important limitations
- Cross-country listed prices do not always share identical tax treatment.
- The primary affordability measure uses household-consumption PPP per capita. It is not salary, median income, or disposable income.
- iPhone 17 moved to 256GB base storage, so 2025 versus older 128GB base models is not a pure like-for-like inflation comparison.
- TCO resale, financing, and repair assumptions are scenarios, not claims about future observed resale values.
- The 13-market comparison is a selected-market sample, not a claim about global rank.
